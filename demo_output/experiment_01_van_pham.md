### Summary
Van Pham presents a mathematical framework for modeling the secondary structures of co-transcriptional R-loops using a 2D tiling system inspired by the board game Carcassonne and Wang tiles. By defining a set of tiles representing DNA and RNA strands and establishing compatibility rules via a master graph, the team generates probable structures termed "combs." The research utilizes rank entropy calculations to predict the most likely R-loop topologies, identifying specific "blob" structures as highly probable while classifying others as less likely.
